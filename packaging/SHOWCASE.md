## 🧭 SCUM MiniMap v2.1.0 — a closer look

Set up the minimap before you start playing: size, shape, opacity, rotation, map layers, player/route/history colours, tracking, zoom and voice. The setup wizard now includes working settings and a live preview, with the option to restore the default colours.

📍 **See your group.** Private Host/Join sharing brings players, active GPS destinations, saved locations and death markers onto your map. Each player has their own visibility pills in the big-map sidebar. Hide what you don’t need without stopping sharing.

🗺️ **Keep the map readable.** Clearer category icons, zoom-aware sizing and optional POI clustering, with individual category filters.

🧭 **Find your way.** A rotating minimap, named off-screen direction arrows, shared navigation targets and owner-labelled markers.

🔗 **Connect directly.** No VPN account or relay service required. Host/Join uses a reachable TCP connection; compatible routers support automatic forwarding, with manual setup available. Public-IP detection stays with your local router, and hosting checks the Windows Firewall exception.

Screenshots show the real app with demonstration players and coordinates. Text UI supports 19 languages. Visibility and sharing sessions are temporary; appearance settings are saved.
